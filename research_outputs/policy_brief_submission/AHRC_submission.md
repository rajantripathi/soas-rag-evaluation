# AHRC Policy Brief Submission Package
## Date: 20 March 2026

## Funding Body: Arts and Humanities Research Council (AHRC)

### Executive Summary for AHRC

**Problem:** AI systems fail in culturally specific contexts because underlying knowledge sources are incomplete.

**Finding:** Knowledge source coverage is the dominant bottleneck. Corpus curation (61 documents) produces 59% improvement (p < 0.001) vs 7.5% from model optimisation.

**Implication:** Fund knowledge curation, not just model scaling.

### Why This Matters for AHRC

**Aligns with AHRC Priorities:**
1. **Cultural heritage preservation:** Validates community-led documentation
2. **Underrepresented languages:** Focus on Uzbek highlights resource gaps  
3. **Methodological innovation:** Statistical rigour sets new standards
4. **Policy relevance:** Direct implications for AI evaluation

**Cost-Effectiveness:**
- £10,000 for corpus curation → 59% improvement
- £100,000+ for model training → 7.5% improvement
- **10x more cost-effective** to curate knowledge
